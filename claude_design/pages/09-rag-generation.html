Lost in the middle

Liu et al. found that LLMs reliably attend to passages at the start and end of a long prompt — and forget the ones in the middle. The accuracy curve is U-shaped. Practically: put your highest-rank passages at positions 1, 2, last; never bury the gold in the middle.

Citation discipline

// production rules
Inline cite-IDs — every claim tied to a passage id the user can click.
Refuse on miss — "I don\'t know" when context lacks the answer (do not let the model fall back on parametric memory).
Reserve budget for refusal — leave ~50 tokens for "I couldn\'t find this" plus the next-step suggestion.
